# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JS. Multi-page (Home, About, Books, book detail, Reviews, Contact, Privacy). No build step.

## Users

- Readers (mostly mothers, often in or past early motherhood) who hear about the memoir and want to know what it is, whether it's for them, and when they can get it.
- Event organizers, book clubs, libraries and podcasts looking to book Becky for an author talk or event.

## Product Purpose

The official author website for Becky Rose (pen name of Becky Stadnick), promoting her debut memoir *Laughing Baby, Crying Mom: On Intrusive Thoughts, Laughter, and Putting Myself Back Together*, released October 30, 2026 (out now). Success: a visitor understands the book's honesty-plus-humor promise, feels seen, and either buys it or reaches out to book an event.

## Positioning

A memoir about postpartum depression, anxiety and intrusive thoughts told with disarming humor — "laughing, then crying" — by a Minnesota mom, crafter and rock hound. Its signature image is "a woman doing her best to show up, night after night, beneath the dim glow of a red nursery light."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The book is out. Buy links: none provided yet. "Buy the book" buttons use dead (`#`) placeholder links, clearly easy to replace.
- Contact form: visual only, no functionality for now. Email shown: wardlow.becky@gmail.com.
- One book only today; Books page should still read as a list that can grow.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Becky Rose. Book cover (assets/cover.webp) is fixed: dark painted abstract ground, hot-pink brush script, white serif caps.
- Author photo: assets/becky.webp (outdoor selfie, red jacket, forest/river).
- Voice: warm, candid, funny, unpolished-on-purpose. Existing copy is kept verbatim.

## Evidence on Hand

- Two reader reviews (Megan F., Melissa C.) — verbatim from the current site.
- Book description and author bio — verbatim from the current site.
- No press quotes, blurbs from known authors, sales figures, publisher name, or event listings exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Make readers feel seen and less alone before anything else.
2. Honesty and humor sit side by side; never sanitize either.
3. The book leads; the site is its companion.
4. Easy for Becky to update later (plain files, obvious placeholders).
